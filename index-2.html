Math Games

Free classroom games for Pre-K through Grade 6. They run in the browser with no ads, accounts, or tracking.

Math Museum

Read each worked problem and decide whether it has a mistake. Students find the error, explain why, and correct it, or choose “No errors.”

  • Order of operations
  • Regrouping
  • Fractions
  • Word problems
  • Mixed

Math Tug of War

Two teams, or one student against the computer, pull the rope with correct answers. The losing team falls in the mud. Teachers can set the time limit and add their own questions.

  • Pre-K to Grade 6
  • + − × ÷
  • Fractions and decimals
  • Patterns and missing numbers
  • Your own questions
