Math Tug of War

Answer correctly to pull the rope toward your team. The first team to pull the rope all the way wins. If time runs out, the team that is ahead wins.

My own questions (optional)

Type one question per line, then an equals sign, then the answer. The answer must be a number: a whole number, a decimal, a fraction such as 3/4, or a negative number. Lines that start with # are ignored.

How are questions given?

How to enter answers
  • Touch screen or interactive whiteboard: tap the number buttons, then the green check button.
  • Computer keyboard: type the answer and press Enter. Backspace deletes.
  • Two teams, one keyboard: the number row and Enter key type for the left team (or whichever team you last clicked). The number pad on the right of the keyboard and its Enter key type for the right team. On a laptop without a number pad, the right team can tap its on-screen buttons.
  • Fractions and decimals (Advanced): use the / and . keys.

This page works offline. It has no ads, accounts, or tracking and never sends anything anywhere. Only your game settings are remembered, and only on this device.
